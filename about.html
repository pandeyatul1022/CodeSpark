Our Story

Bridging the gap between theory and hands-on creation

Traditional coding education often feels intimidating, heavy on syntax rules, and light on practical application. CodeSpark was born out of a desire to change that narrative.

We believe everyone can learn to code when provided with bite-sized lessons, instant feedback loops, and a supportive playground to experiment without fear of making mistakes.

Interactive First Learning
